### Quality Assurance and Screening Protocols
1. **Coordinate Verification:** All sampling stations were georeferenced against documented coordinates or named geographic landmarks and snapped to HydroSHEDS HydroRIVERS river centerlines (mean offset: 357 m).
2. **Instrumentation Standards:** Studies utilized certified atomic absorption spectrophotometry (AAS, GF-AAS) or inductively coupled plasma mass spectrometry (ICP-MS, Agilent 7500/7700 series).
3. **Traceability:** Raw spreadsheet matrices were audited against original published article PDF tables to resolve transcription and fill-down errors.
