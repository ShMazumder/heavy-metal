### Overview and Key Contributions
Surface freshwater networks across the Bengal Delta provide vital resources for regional agriculture, capture fisheries, and municipal water abstraction. Although numerous localized field studies have examined trace elements in Bangladeshi rivers, comprehensive synthesis of benchmark exceedances, rigorous evaluation of seasonal contrasts, and quantification of spatial surveillance coverage have remained fragmented.

In this revision (Round 3 / Critical Revision), we have systematically addressed all reviewer remarks and incorporated extensive methodological, regulatory, and conceptual refinements:
1. **Title and Scope Reframing:** Retitled the manuscript to frankly acknowledge that published literature characterizes **monitored industrial corridors** under intense anthropogenic pressure, rather than an unbiased census of national baseline waters.
2. **Regulatory Benchmark Discordance (ECR 1997 vs. ECR 2023):** Systematically benchmarked records against both historical ECR 1997 (Schedule 3) and contemporary ECR 2023 (Schedule 2; S.R.O. 53-Law/2023). We document that under the tightened ECR 2023 standards, national Lead violation jumps from **34.9% to 85.6%** (harmonizing with WHO), Cadmium from **40.2% to 44.7%**, and Nickel from **45.2% to 61.5%**, completely overturning earlier notions of broad domestic statutory compliance.
3. **Operational Fraction Confounder (Confirmed Dissolved Records):** Evaluated sensitivity strictly on confirmed dissolved-phase records (0.45 $\mu$m filtered; $k=10$ studies, $N=131$--$139$): Lead WHO exceedance remains **84.0%** (cluster 95% CI: 66.2--93.5%), Chromium **55.4%**, Arsenic **52.4%**, Cadmium **37.6%**, and Nickel **47.2%**, refuting that elevated concentrations are artifacts of particulate sediment digestion.
4. **Mathematical Artifacts in HPI and Quadratic Scaling Proof:** Provided an algebraic proof demonstrating that HPI sub-index weighting scales quadratically with $1/S_i^2$ ($100 \sum M_i / S_i^2 / \sum 1/S_i$), magnifying Cadmium's weighting advantage over Zinc by $10^6$ (a factor of 1,000,000) and concentrating **78.3% of Core-4 index weight** onto Cd (60.2%) and Pb (18.1%). This rigorously explains the apparent panel invariance across Core-4 (254.13), Core-5 (253.38), and All-7 (253.02) on identical common subsets ($N=35$; full $N=112$ Core-4 median: 156.99) as an arithmetic damping artifact. We promote unweighted complementary indices (HEI median 6.70; mean hazard quotient $\text{HQ}_{\text{mean}} = 1.68$).
5. **Seasonal Contrasts, Simpson's Paradox, and HKSJ Meta-Analysis:** Explained the discrepancy between pooled multi-study comparisons and within-study paired contrasts via Simpson's Paradox (cross-study spatial imbalance). Applied the Hartung-Knapp-Sidik-Jonkman (HKSJ) adjustment to paired meta-analyses ($k=4$), yielding conservative 95% CIs (Pb: 1.28× [0.56--2.95]; As: 1.43× [0.60--3.41]) that acknowledge small-sample limitations, and identified the monsoon agricultural runoff driver behind Copper seasonal reversal (0.54×).
6. **Resolution of Arsenic Median Paradox:** Documented the tripartite hydrological distribution: pre-monsoon summer irrigation pumping ($N=41$, median **0.0304 mg/L**, mean 0.0632 mg/L) represents the annual concentration maximum, pulling the pooled median (0.0152 mg/L) above both dry winter (0.0073 mg/L) and wet monsoon (0.0042 mg/L) levels.
7. **Spatial Surveillance Deficits and Estuarine Dynamics:** Maintained HydroSHEDS topological stream routing proving that compiled sites cover only **16.5% of major river length (83.5% uncovered at 25 km straight-line; 89.0% along-network)**, confirmed by Monte Carlo testing ($p < 0.0001$). Clarified that candidate monitoring reaches in dynamic estuarine channels (`GAP_STN_01` Chandpur confluence) require multi-point depth-integrated cross-channel transects across tidal phases, and plotted the 15 prioritized candidate stations on the national network map.
8. **PRISMA Literature Transparency and Dedicated Graphical Abstract:** Formally incorporated PRISMA search flow accounting (1,420 hits $\to$ 74 full text $\to$ 28 eligible; 11 bed sediment allocated to companion audit, 5 bulk physicochemical cataloged in Table S0, and 12 primary heavy metal field studies cited directly with discrete station observation clustering). Designed a comprehensive 4-panel Graphical Abstract highlighting the regulatory delta, HPI quadratic weighting artifact, seasonal As hydrochemistry, and network surveillance gaps.
