### Overview and Key Contributions
Surface freshwater networks across the Bengal Delta provide vital resources for regional agriculture, capture fisheries, and domestic intake. Although numerous localized field studies have examined trace elements in Bangladeshi rivers, comprehensive synthesis of benchmark exceedances, rigorous evaluation of seasonal contrasts, and quantification of spatial surveillance coverage have remained fragmented.

In this study, we present a systematic multi-study synthesis of 268 published river water records across 12 river systems from 28 peer-reviewed field investigations (2010--2022):
1. **Benchmark Exceedance Synthesis:** Audited concentrations against WHO reference drinking guidelines (4th ed., 2022 addenda) and Bangladesh ECR statutory standards, calculating 95% Wilson score confidence intervals. Lead (Pb) exceeded the WHO guideline (0.010 mg/L) in **85.6%** of published records (95% CI: 79.0--90.4%, median: 0.0286 mg/L, max: 0.700 mg/L), while total Chromium (60.6%), Arsenic (54.9%), and Nickel (54.8%) also exhibited frequent WHO guideline exceedances.
2. **Hotspot Sensitivity Analysis:** Excluding peri-urban industrial rivers (Buriganga, Turag, Shitalakhya) showed lower national exceedances (e.g., Bangladesh standard exceedance for Pb fell from 34.9% to 18.3%, and Arsenic from 18.0% to 8.9%), empirically demonstrating that literature-derived metrics are strongly shaped by metropolitan industrial outfalls.
3. **Multi-Metal Pollution Indices:** Evaluation of the Heavy Metal Pollution Index ($\mathrm{HPI}$) across multi-metal records yielded a median of **202.30** (IQR: 79.01--793.90, mean: 718.74), with 63.1% exceeding the critical threshold of 100 and highest loadings concentrated in the Meghna, Buriganga, and Shitalakhya rivers.
4. **Seasonal Contrasts with Multiplicity Correction:** Two-sided Mann-Whitney U hypothesis testing with family-wise Holm-Bonferroni correction confirmed statistically significant seasonal dilution for Arsenic (dry median 0.0073 mg/L vs. wet median 0.0042 mg/L; 1.74× ratio, $p = 0.0039$, $p_{\mathrm{adj}} = 0.0273$). In contrast, Lead showed no seasonal dilution ($p = 0.8538$), pointing to compensatory wet-season diffuse urban stormwater wash-off.
5. **Spatial Surveillance Gap Analysis:** Geodesic distance routing across HydroSHEDS HydroRIVERS established that **87.5% of major river reach length (7,372.3 km across 3,408 reaches)** is located $>25$ km away from any published sampling location. We provide 15 prioritized, geographically dispersed surveillance candidate reaches spaced $\ge 30$ km apart across the Lower Meghna, Padma, and Jamuna river basins.

This manuscript falls directly within the scope of *Chemosphere* (Environmental Chemistry and Aquatic Environment section), providing a calibrated, evidence-based synthesis of trace element distributions, benchmark exceedance rates, and seasonal hydrochemical dynamics in a major deltaic river system.
